What we do

Four things that make life in China work

Not a chat group you mute after a week — a real-life community that answers your 11pm China-life question and then meets you for dumplings on Saturday.

01

Daily Crew Communication

Online groups where expats swap first-hand answers about daily life in China — housing, healthcare, banking, mobile payments and everything in between. Peer advice from people who have already done it, not a service desk.

02

Chinese Language Exchange and Learning

Language exchange is the third of our ten formats. It puts expats who want Mandarin next to locals who want English, and it gives anyone a stated reason to turn up.

03

Organization of Social Activities

Ten formats on the board, from bar nights and pub crawls, food crawls and language exchange through to sports groups, hiking trips and Chinese culture workshops.

04

Cultural Experience Activities

Culture workshops sit sixth of our ten formats, the highest of the group that needs organising before anything happens. Interest in Chinese culture is common; acting on it alone is harder, because you have to find someone who teaches it, get to the right part of town and then work out what you are looking at. A workshop handles all three.

Cities & guides

Where the crew meets

14 cities covered by guides and online groups. Guangzhou and Shenzhen are where we meet in person.

Guangzhou

Our home base — Cantonese food, riverfront nights and the easiest city to make friends in.

Shanghai

China’s most international city — the biggest expat crowd and the widest choice of things to do.

Beijing

History, hutongs and the Great Wall within reach.

Shenzhen

Tech energy, coastline and the youngest crowd in the Pearl River Delta.

Chengdu

Chengdu runs at a slower pace than the coastal cities, and its teahouses, mahjong tables and Sichuan food are local institutions rather than tourist attractions. The crew meets in person in Guangzhou and Shenzhen, so members here join the national online groups and the Chengdu city chat.

Hangzhou

Hangzhou has West Lake, tea hills on the edge of the city and a tech scene that keeps pulling newcomers in, so the crowd here skews young and recently arrived. The crew meets in person in Guangzhou and Shenzhen, so members here join the national online groups and the Hangzhou city chat.

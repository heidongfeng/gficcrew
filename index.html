What we do

Four things that make life in China work

Not a chat group you mute after a week — a real-life community that answers your 11pm China-life question and then meets you for dumplings on Saturday.

01

Daily Crew Communication

Online groups where expats swap first-hand answers about daily life in China — housing, healthcare, banking, mobile payments and everything in between. Peer advice from people who have already done it, not a service desk.

02

Chinese Language Exchange and Learning

Break the language barrier with language exchange meetups, survival Chinese crash courses, HSK preparation groups and Chinese corner events.

03

Organization of Social Activities

Meetups fall into three main formats — social mixers and parties, food crawls and dining, and hiking and city walks — plus sports socials and member-driven meetups.

04

Cultural Experience Activities

Dive deep into Chinese heritage with your crew — calligraphy, tea ceremonies, tai chi, Mahjong nights and guided tours well beyond the tourist path.

Cities & guides

Where the crew meets

14 cities covered by guides and online groups. Guangzhou and Shenzhen are where we meet in person.

Guangzhou

Our home base — Cantonese food, riverfront nights and the easiest city to make friends in.

Shanghai

China’s most international city — rooftop parties, business mixers and art weekends.

Beijing

History, hutongs and the Great Wall on your doorstep.

Shenzhen

Tech energy, coastline and a young international crowd.

Chengdu

Chengdu is famous for its relaxed pace, teahouse culture and spicy Sichuan food — and our crew leans into all three. Expect hotpot nights and teahouse socials, Sichuan food crawls, and unhurried city walks with the occasional hill hike outside town.

Hangzhou

West Lake, tea hills and a booming tech scene. Our Hangzhou crew is an outdoor-first group: lakeside socials and weekend brunches, food crawls through the old streets, and walks and runs around West Lake and up into the tea mountains.

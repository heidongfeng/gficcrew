Brandy's WildFest 2026 in Guangzhou

Join GFIC Crew member Brandy for WildFest 2026, a two-day music, workshop and camping weekend in Baiyun District, Guangzhou, on 14–15 November.

One of the best things about our crew is seeing members bring their own projects to life. This November, GFIC Crew member Brandy is hosting the third annual ToughCookie's WildFest, and we want to see you there. It is a two-day community-driven weekend focused on creativity, culture and connection. Expect creative workshops, live music jams, comedy performances, interactive games, and a "Wild Swap" market where you can trade things you no longer need. There will be a bonfire at night and optional camping under the stars. THE DETAILS Dates: November 14–15, 2026 (Saturday and Sunday) Location: Blue Island Campground, Liaocai Yanhe Road, Zhongluotan Town, Baiyun District, Guangzhou Tickets: ¥69 advance for both days; ¥99 at the door. The first 99 buyers get a small gift. Children: free for ages 12 and under with a ticketed adult. The event is family-friendly; organizers suggest putting younger children to bed after 10pm when the grown-ups get louder. Pets: well-behaved pets are welcome on a leash; please clean up after them. CAMPING OPTIONS (separate from the festival ticket) Survival Mode (¥29): reserved ground space; bring your own gear. Happy Camper (¥99): reserved space plus a simple two-person tent. Glamper (¥599): tent, air mattress, sleeping bags, pillows, lantern, flameless mini BBQ and marshmallows. CAN I COME ALONE? Yes. Many people do. The interactive games and workshops are designed to break the ice naturally, so no awkward small talk is required. Come for a few hours, stay all day, or spend the night; camping is entirely optional. If you have questions, ask Brandy directly in the group.

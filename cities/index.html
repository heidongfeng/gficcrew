Guangzhou

Guangdong · Most active chapter · Guide available

Our home base — Cantonese food, riverfront nights and the easiest city to make friends in.

Guangzhou is where GFIC Crew is most active. It is one of the most expat-friendly cities in China: affordable rent, world-class Cantonese food, a subtropical climate and a deeply international business community. Our Guangzhou crew runs weekly language exchange, monthly food crawls in Liwan and Yuexiu, badminton socials and weekend trips to the surrounding countryside.

  • Weekly Cantonese and Mandarin language exchange
  • Street food crawls through Liwan, Yuexiu and Haizhu
  • Badminton, football and cycling socials
  • Cantonese dim sum workshops and tea ceremony sessions

Cost of living: Monthly living cost for a single expat is roughly RMB 6,000–10,000 including a one-bedroom apartment in Yuexiu, Tianhe or Zhujiang New Town.

Shanghai

Shanghai · Most active chapter

China’s most international city — rooftop parties, business mixers and art weekends.

Shanghai has the largest expat community in mainland China. Our Shanghai crew focuses on neighbourhood-based meetups in Jing’an, Xuhui and Huangpu, plus business networking mixers and museum weekends.

  • Neighbourhood coffee chats and pub crawls
  • Business and freelancer networking mixers
  • Art gallery and museum weekends
  • Weekend trips to Hangzhou and Suzhou

Cost of living: Expect RMB 10,000–18,000 per month for a comfortable single lifestyle with an apartment inside the inner ring.

Beijing

Beijing · Most active chapter

History, hutongs and the Great Wall on your doorstep.

Beijing offers unmatched access to Chinese history. Our crew organizes hutong walks, Great Wall trekking days, hotpot feasts and embassy-area socials.

  • Great Wall trekking day trips
  • Hutong and temple guided walks
  • Hotpot and Peking duck feasts
  • Language exchange in Chaoyang and Dongcheng

Cost of living: A single expat typically spends RMB 8,000–14,000 per month, with apartments in Chaoyang and Dongcheng most popular.

Shenzhen

Guangdong · Most active chapter

Tech energy, coastline and a young international crowd.

Shenzhen is China’s tech hub and one of its youngest cities. Our Shenzhen crew runs hiking socials along the coastline, startup mixers and Cantonese food nights.

  • Coastal hikes and beach days
  • Tech and startup mixers
  • Cantonese seafood dinners
  • Cycling along Dapeng

Cost of living: Budget RMB 8,000–14,000 per month; Nanshan and Futian are the most popular districts.

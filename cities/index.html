Guangzhou

Guangdong · Most active chapter · Guide available

Our home base — Cantonese food, riverfront nights and the easiest city to make friends in.

Guangzhou is where GFIC Crew is most active, and it is one of the most expat-friendly cities in China: affordable rent, world-class Cantonese food, a subtropical climate and a deeply international business community. Our Guangzhou crew is an events crew first — social mixers and riverfront parties, street-food crawls through Liwan and Yuexiu, and weekend hikes and city walks around Baiyun Mountain, with language exchange and badminton running alongside them all week.

  • Social mixers & parties: riverfront pub crawls in Zhujiang New Town, international potluck dinners and rooftop meetups
  • Food crawls & dining: Liwan, Yuexiu and Haizhu street-food crawls plus Cantonese dim sum and tea ceremony sessions
  • Hiking & city walks: Baiyun Mountain sunrise hikes, Shamian Island and old-town walks, weekend camping in Conghua
  • Weekly Cantonese and Mandarin language exchange
  • Badminton, football and cycling socials
  • Weekend trips to the surrounding countryside

Cost of living: Monthly living cost for a single expat is roughly RMB 6,000–10,000 including a one-bedroom apartment in Yuexiu, Tianhe or Zhujiang New Town.

Shanghai

Shanghai · Most active chapter

China’s most international city — rooftop parties, business mixers and art weekends.

Shanghai has the largest expat community in mainland China. Our Shanghai crew is built around three things: social mixers and rooftop parties, food crawls from street stalls to tasting menus, and city walks that actually explain the city — alongside neighbourhood meetups in Jing’an, Xuhui and Huangpu.

  • Social mixers & parties: rooftop mixers, business and freelancer networking nights, pub crawls
  • Food crawls & dining: night-market and noodle-shop crawls, supper clubs and neighbourhood dining runs
  • Hiking & city walks: Bund-to-French-Concession walks, art-district strolls and day hikes in the hills outside town
  • Neighbourhood coffee chats in Jing’an and Xuhui
  • Art gallery and museum weekends
  • Weekend trips to Hangzhou and Suzhou

Cost of living: Expect RMB 10,000–18,000 per month for a comfortable single lifestyle with an apartment inside the inner ring.

Beijing

Beijing · Most active chapter

History, hutongs and the Great Wall on your doorstep.

Beijing offers unmatched access to Chinese history, and our crew explores it on foot. Expect social mixers and hotpot dinners, hutong food crawls, and the hiking this region is famous for — Great Wall treks and city walks that take in temples, hutongs and lakesides.

  • Social mixers & parties: embassy-area socials, hotpot nights and international mixers
  • Food crawls & dining: hutong snack crawls, Peking duck feasts and Muslim-Quarter dining runs
  • Hiking & city walks: Great Wall trekking days, hutong and temple guided walks, lakeside strolls
  • Language exchange in Chaoyang and Dongcheng
  • Museum and art-district weekends
  • Weekend trips to the surrounding mountains

Cost of living: A single expat typically spends RMB 8,000–14,000 per month, with apartments in Chaoyang and Dongcheng most popular.

Shenzhen

Guangdong · Most active chapter

Tech energy, coastline and a young international crowd.

Shenzhen is China’s tech hub and one of its youngest cities, and the crew here moves outdoors. We run social mixers and startup parties, Cantonese seafood crawls along the coast, and some of the best coastal hikes and city walks in the country.

  • Social mixers & parties: startup and tech mixers, beach bonfires and rooftop meetups
  • Food crawls & dining: Cantonese seafood dinners, night-market crawls and hotpot nights
  • Hiking & city walks: coastal hikes and beach days, Dapeng cycling and urban-park walks
  • Language exchange for newcomers
  • Weekend trips around the Pearl River Delta
  • Board-game and photography meetups

Cost of living: Budget RMB 8,000–14,000 per month; Nanshan and Futian are the most popular districts.

Chengdu

Sichuan

Chengdu is famous for its relaxed pace, teahouse culture and spicy Sichuan food — and our crew leans into all three. Expect hotpot nights and teahouse socials, Sichuan food crawls, and unhurried city walks with the occasional hill hike outside town.

  • Social mixers & parties: hotpot nights, teahouse afternoons and Mahjong learning socials
  • Food crawls & dining: Sichuan cuisine tours, street-skewer crawls and dumpling nights
  • Hiking & city walks: old-town and riverside walks, with day hikes in the hills around Chengdu
  • Panda base trips
  • Language exchange and cultural workshops
  • Weekend trips to Qingcheng Mountain and nearby towns

Cost of living: One of the most affordable big cities in China — RMB 5,000–9,000 per month is comfortable.

Hangzhou

Zhejiang

West Lake, tea hills and a booming tech scene. Our Hangzhou crew is an outdoor-first group: lakeside socials and weekend brunches, food crawls through the old streets, and walks and runs around West Lake and up into the tea mountains.

  • Social mixers & parties: weekend brunch club, lakeside socials and tech-community mixers
  • Food crawls & dining: Hangzhou-cuisine crawls and Longjing tea-house dining
  • Hiking & city walks: West Lake walks and runs, tea-hill hikes and old-street strolls
  • Longjing tea-picking days
  • Language exchange and cultural workshops
  • Weekend trips to Shanghai and Wuzhen water town

Cost of living: Budget RMB 7,000–12,000 per month.

Chongqing

Chongqing

A vertical city of hotpot, fog and river views, and a great place to explore on foot — though "walk" here often means stairs. Our crew runs social mixers and nightlife nights, spicy food crawls, and night-view walks and hill hikes across the city.

  • Social mixers & parties: nightlife socials, river-cruise parties and hotpot gatherings
  • Food crawls & dining: hotpot food crawls and Chongqing small-noodle dining runs
  • Hiking & city walks: night-view photography walks, hillside stair walks and karst day hikes
  • River cruises on the Yangtze and Jialing
  • Language exchange and cultural workshops
  • Weekend trips to Wulong karst country

Cost of living: Very affordable: RMB 5,000–8,500 per month.

Suzhou

Jiangsu

Classical gardens, water towns and a strong international manufacturing community close to Shanghai. Our Suzhou crew covers all three activity types: garden-side socials and mixers, Jiangnan food crawls, and the canal, garden and water-town walks this city is made for.

  • Social mixers & parties: garden-side socials, expat mixers and weekend brunches
  • Food crawls & dining: Jiangnan cuisine crawls, noodle-shop runs and sweet-shop tastings
  • Hiking & city walks: classical garden tours, canal-side walks and water-town strolls
  • Silk and embroidery workshops
  • Shanghai–Suzhou cycling
  • Weekend trips to Tongli and Zhouzhuang

Cost of living: Budget RMB 6,000–10,000 per month.

Xi’an

Shaanxi

Terracotta warriors, Muslim Quarter street food and one of the most walkable old cities in China. Our Xi’an crew runs social mixers and courtyard nights, a serious street-food crawl scene, and city walks along the wall and through the old quarters.

  • Social mixers & parties: courtyard socials, city-wall sunset meetups and international mixers
  • Food crawls & dining: Muslim Quarter food walks, biangbiang noodle crawls and dumpling banquets
  • Hiking & city walks: city-wall cycling and walks, old-quarter strolls and day hikes in the Qinling mountains
  • Terracotta Army trips
  • Calligraphy workshops
  • Language exchange and cultural workshops

Cost of living: Budget RMB 5,000–8,000 per month.

Tianjin

Tianjin

A port city 30 minutes from Beijing by high-speed rail, with European-style concessions and a growing international community. Our Tianjin crew runs port-area socials and coffee meetups, street-food crawls, and concession-architecture walks along the Hai River.

  • Social mixers & parties: port-area coffee meetups, expat mixers and riverside socials
  • Food crawls & dining: Tianjin street-food nights, goubuli and mahua tastings
  • Hiking & city walks: concession-architecture walks, Hai River promenades and Panshan day hikes
  • Beijing–Tianjin day trips
  • Language exchange and cultural workshops
  • Weekend trips to the Bohai coast

Cost of living: Budget RMB 5,500–9,000 per month.

Guilin

Guangxi

Karst mountains and the Li River, and a slower, nature-first base popular with teachers, photographers and remote workers. Our Guilin crew is outdoors-led: social mixers and photography nights, local food crawls, and karst hiking and river walks.

  • Social mixers & parties: photography socials, hostel and coworking mixers, riverside evenings
  • Food crawls & dining: Guilin rice-noodle crawls and local night-market dining
  • Hiking & city walks: karst hiking, Li River walks and rural cycling
  • Language exchange and cultural workshops
  • Photography weekends
  • Weekend trips to Yangshuo and Longji rice terraces

Cost of living: Budget RMB 4,000–7,000 per month.

Yunnan (Kunming, Dali, Lijiang)

Yunnan

Spring-like weather, minority cultures and a strong remote-work community across Kunming, Dali and Lijiang. Our Yunnan crew runs social mixers and festival parties, Yunnan food crawls, and the lake, mountain and old-town walks this province is famous for.

  • Social mixers & parties: remote-worker mixers, festival parties and old-town socials in Dali and Lijiang
  • Food crawls & dining: Yunnan cuisine crawls, mushroom-season dining and market tours
  • Hiking & city walks: Erhai lake cycling and walks, Cangshan hiking and Lijiang old-town strolls
  • Minority festival trips
  • Tea mountain visits
  • Language exchange and cultural workshops

Cost of living: Budget RMB 4,000–8,000 per month depending on the city.

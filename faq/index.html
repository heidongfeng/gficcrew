About GFIC Crew

What is GFIC Crew (GFIC歪果社)?

GFIC Crew (GFIC歪果社) is a growing real-life social community for expats in China. Founded by passionate locals who know every corner of this country, we are dedicated to helping global friends live a fun, connected and hassle-free life here. We organize real-world events — themed parties, food crawls, sports leagues, hikes, cultural workshops, language exchanges and weekend getaways — in Guangzhou, Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen, Chengdu and 11 more cities.

Who can join GFIC Crew?

Anyone living in China as an expat, and anyone planning to move to China who wants a community before they land. That includes English teachers, engineers, entrepreneurs, students, remote workers, trailing spouses and long-term travellers. We also welcome globally-minded Chinese members who want genuine international friendships. There is no nationality requirement.

Is GFIC Crew active in Guangzhou?

Yes. Guangzhou is our priority city and our most active chapter. The Guangzhou crew runs a weekly Cantonese and Mandarin language exchange every Wednesday, weekly badminton socials, a monthly Liwan street food crawl, monthly international potlucks and riverfront pub crawls in Zhujiang New Town, plus quarterly camping trips to Conghua.

Which cities does GFIC Crew cover?

We are currently active in Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Hangzhou, Chongqing, Chengdu, Guilin, Yunnan (Kunming, Dali, Lijiang), Suzhou, Qingdao, Xi’an, Tianjin and Xiamen. We also maintain pop-up chapters and organize trips to other expat-frequented destinations across China.

How is GFIC Crew different from other expat groups in China?

Most expat groups in China are either a chat group with no real-world meetups, or a commercial ticket seller. GFIC Crew is built around recurring, crew-led real-life events and practical mutual support: housing, banking and mobile payments answered by people who have already done it. Membership is lifetime, and there are no commercial advertisements inside the crew.

Membership & Fees

How much does GFIC Crew membership cost?

Lifetime membership is RMB 288 (USD 42) for the National Group, RMB 688 (USD 98) for a City Group and RMB 2,888 (USD 420) for the Business Group. It is a one-time fee, not a subscription.

What is the difference between the National Group, City Group and Business Group?

The National Group (RMB 288) gives you access to the nationwide community, online groups and event announcements. A City Group (RMB 688) adds the local chapter of your choice with its own weekly and monthly events, local guides and housing support. The Business Group (RMB 2,888) adds business networking, partner introductions and visibility for your own projects within the crew.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Members who have not participated in any activities within a week of joining the crew and have not violated the community rules may receive a full refund of their membership fee if they leave.

Do I need to become a member to attend an event?

Most events are open to non-members at a drop-in price, and some community events are free. Members pay less or nothing and get first access to limited-capacity events such as camping trips and Great Wall trekking days. You can attend once before deciding.

How do I pay the membership fee?

After you submit the join form on this website or message us on WeChat (DN8117), we send payment instructions by WeChat Pay or Alipay. Once payment is confirmed you are added to the relevant city group within 24 hours.

Events & Activities

What kind of events does GFIC Crew organize?

Five recurring formats: themed social gatherings (international potlucks, pub crawls, board game nights, holiday parties), sports and fitness socials (badminton, football, basketball, cycling, yoga, city hikes), food exploration (street food walks, hotpot feasts, dumpling-making classes, craft beer tastings, regional cuisine tours), outdoor adventures (weekend camping, Great Wall trekking, river tracing, rural cycling) and member-driven meetups (coffee chats, photography walks, book clubs).

How often are events held?

In Guangzhou there is at least one crew event every week, usually a language exchange on Wednesday and a badminton social on Tuesday, plus three to four larger weekend events each month. Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen and Chengdu run two to four events per month. Smaller chapters run monthly events and join regional trips.

How do I sign up for an event?

Create a free account on this website, open the Events page, choose an event and submit the sign-up form. We confirm by email and add you to the event WeChat group a day before it starts. Members can also message us directly on WeChat (DN8117).

Can I organize my own event through GFIC Crew?

Yes, and we encourage it. Community members can initiate their own meetups and the GFIC Crew team offers organizational support and collaboration: venue suggestions, promotion to the city group, and help with booking. Many of our most popular recurring events started as a member’s one-off idea.

Are events suitable for people who do not speak Chinese?

Yes. Every event has at least one bilingual host, and announcements are published in English. Events are also the fastest way to pick up practical Chinese, and our language exchange is designed for absolute beginners.

Can I bring friends, partners or children?

Partners and friends are welcome at social and food events. Some outdoor and sports events have age or fitness limits, which are stated in each event description. Family-friendly events are labelled in the calendar.

Language & Culture

Do you offer Chinese language exchange?

Yes. We facilitate language exchange meetups, survival Chinese crash courses, HSK preparation groups and Chinese corner events. You can find a tandem partner to practice Mandarin and Cantonese, share learning tips and level up in a supportive, fun environment.

Can I learn Cantonese with GFIC Crew?

Yes, especially in Guangzhou and Shenzhen where Cantonese is part of daily life. Our Guangzhou language exchange splits each session between Mandarin and Cantonese, and our dim sum and tea workshops are taught with Cantonese phrases included.

What cultural activities do you organize?

Calligraphy and ink painting workshops, traditional tea ceremonies, tai chi and kung fu introductions, Mahjong learning nights, and guided museum or historical neighbourhood tours. We also arrange regional cultural immersions such as Suzhou water towns, Yunnan minority festivals, lantern festivals and temple fairs.

Living in China — Housing

How much does rent cost in Guangzhou?

As a general guide, a modern one-bedroom in Zhujiang New Town costs RMB 4,000–7,000 per month, a comparable apartment in Tianhe or Yuexiu RMB 3,000–5,500, and a share-flat room RMB 1,500–2,800. Prices are roughly half of Shanghai and Beijing for equivalent quality.

Living in China — Money

How do I set up WeChat Pay and Alipay as a foreigner?

You need a Chinese bank account or an accepted international card, then verify your identity inside WeChat (Me → Services → Wallet → Cards) or Alipay with your passport. Most expats open a local bank account first because it makes rent, utilities and transfers much simpler. Our members share which banks currently accept passport-only applications in each city.

Living in China — Healthcare

Do I need health insurance in China?

Most work permits require it, and it is strongly recommended in any case because private care is expensive. Typically you are covered by employer-provided insurance plus a personal international policy. We share comparisons of local and international plans in the member groups.

Living in China — Daily Life

How do I get a Chinese phone number?

Go to a China Mobile, China Unicom or China Telecom flagship store with your passport; registration is tied to your passport and takes about 15 minutes. You need a number for almost everything — bank accounts, delivery apps, ride hailing, WeChat Pay verification.

Which apps do I need to live in China?

The essentials are WeChat (messaging and payment), Alipay (payment), Amap or Baidu Maps (navigation), Didi (taxis), Meituan or Ele.me (food delivery), Taobao and JD (shopping), 12306 (trains) and Trip.com (flights and hotels).

Guangzhou

Why do expats choose Guangzhou?

Guangzhou combines a lower cost of living than Beijing or Shanghai with world-class Cantonese food, a subtropical climate, a huge international trade community and fast rail links to Hong Kong, Shenzhen and Southeast Asia. It is also one of the friendliest Chinese cities for newcomers because the local culture is famously practical and non-judgemental.

What is the best area to live in Guangzhou as an expat?

Zhujiang New Town is the modern CBD with high-rise apartments and nightlife; Tianhe is convenient and busy; Yuexiu has the old-city feel, parks and the best traditional food; Haizhu is greener and cheaper along the river; Panyu is popular with families near international schools.

Community Rules

What are the GFIC Crew community rules?

Respect the diverse cultures of all countries with no discriminatory remarks based on race, gender, religion or any other grounds; no commercial advertisements; no illegal activities or remarks; and do not harass or add other crew members as friends without their consent. Minor violations get a warning, serious violations mean removal with no refund.

Can I promote my business in the crew?

Commercial advertising inside the community groups is prohibited to keep the atmosphere supportive. Business visibility is what the Business Group membership (RMB 2,888) is for, and we also run partner events where promotion is clearly labelled and agreed in advance.

About GFIC Crew (GFIC歪果社)

Who this is for

Newly arrived expats

Your first month in China is the hardest: SIM card, bank account, WeChat Pay, rental deposit, residence permit. The crew compresses that learning curve from months to weeks.

Long-term residents

You know the systems but the social circle has thinned out. Ten formats to choose from and a rotating cast of newcomers keep the city interesting again.

Planning a move to China

Join before you land. Ask the city group about districts, salaries, schools and contracts, and arrive with people already expecting you.

Globally-minded locals

Chinese members who want genuine international friendships, language practice and a non-commercial space are welcome.

Our four services

Organization of Social Activities

We organize and share social events across China, so no expat has to spend a weekend sitting at home. Ten formats sit on the board: from rooftop parties, BBQs, karaoke nights, wine tastings and club nights to networking mixers, food crawls, hikes, sports leagues and weekend getaways. We pick the place, set the time and gather the crew; all you have to do is turn up.

  • Parties and socials: rooftop parties, BBQs, karaoke nights, wine tastings and club nights
  • Networking mixers, food crawls and shared meals, hikes, sports leagues and weekend getaways
  • Board games and trivia nights, coffee and brunch, interest groups like books, photography and film

Cultural Experience Activities

Culture workshops are the ones that need organizing before anything happens, and that is exactly why they are worth doing with a crew. Interest in Chinese culture is common; acting on it alone is harder, because you have to find someone who teaches it, get to the right part of town and then work out what you are looking at. A workshop handles all three.

  • Chinese cooking sessions, dumplings included
  • Calligraphy and ink painting
  • Tea ceremony, explained by someone who grew up with it
  • Tai chi introductions
  • Museum and temple visits with somebody explaining what you are actually looking at

Chinese Language Exchange and Learning

Language exchange puts expats who want Mandarin next to locals who want English, and it gives anyone a stated reason to turn up.

  • Language exchange meetups with tandem partners
  • Everyday Mandarin for the things you do in your first month
  • Structured practice rather than an open room where nobody talks to anyone new
  • Sessions shaped around a topic instead of free-form mingling
  • Mandarin and Cantonese practice in a supportive environment

Daily Crew Communication

Online groups where expats swap first-hand answers about daily life in China: housing, healthcare, banking, mobile payments and everything in between. Peer advice from people who have already done it, not a service desk.

  • Healthcare: comparing international clinics and health insurance with people who use them
  • Housing: comparing districts, rents and lease terms before you sign
  • Banking and mobile payments: walking each other through WeChat Pay and Alipay setup
  • Other paperwork people get stuck on: driving license conversion and moving pets

How events get organized

  1. Co-creation and execution through third-party partnerships: partner events are planned for later, with the GFIC Crew team providing organizational support.
  2. Initiation and execution by community members: the GFIC Crew team offers organizational support and collaboration.
  3. Initiation, planning and execution by the GFIC Crew team: the team takes the lead in organizing and carrying out events.

Community rules

  1. Show respect for the diverse cultures of all countries and prohibit any discriminatory remarks based on race, gender, religion, or any other grounds.
  2. Prohibit commercial advertisements to ensure a positive and supportive atmosphere within the crew.
  3. Strictly forbid any illegal activities or remarks.
  4. Don't harass or add other crew members as friends without their consent.

Anyone who violates any of the above rules will receive a warning for minor offenses and may be removed from the crew for serious violations.

About this term: expats

You will sometimes see the phrase “foreigners in China” used on this site for broader search coverage. Our preferred and primary term for our community is expats: people who have chosen to build a life here, not just pass through.
